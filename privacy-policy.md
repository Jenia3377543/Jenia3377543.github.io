# Privacy Policy — Theory Champion (אלוף התאוריה)

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

This policy explains what information the Android app **Theory Champion** (package
`com.study.alufhatheoriya`, "the app") handles, why, and what choices you have. The app is
developed by **AlufHaTheoriya** ("we", "us"). Contact: **support.alufhatheoriya@gmail.com**.

## 1. Short version

- You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or any other
  personal details.
- Your study data (answers, progress, exam history, study plan, settings) stays **on your
  device**. We don't have a server that receives it.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device identifiers and usage
  data to show and measure ads, as described below.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on your phone only:

- your answers to practice questions, mistakes for review, and progress per topic;
- mock exam results and history;
- your study plan (exam date, daily study time);
- settings such as study language, license category and whether onboarding is complete.

We can't see this data. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If
Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your
device backup, which Google stores encrypted under your account (see Google's privacy policy).

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland
Limited. To serve, limit the frequency of, measure, and protect ads against fraud, the Google
Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and app-set ID;
- IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
- device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language);
- ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic information (e.g. SDK errors, performance).

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. How Google uses it:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>. Google's privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

**Your choices**

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other regions where required:** on first launch the app
  shows Google's consent form. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your
  choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy choices** inside the app.
- **Everyone:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads,
  in your Android settings (**Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**,
  depending on the device).

If you refuse consent, the app may show non-personalised or limited ads, or no ads.

## 4. Contacting us

**Settings → Report bug** opens our bug report page, which uses a Google Forms form. We
receive only what you type into it (for example a description of the problem and, if you
choose to give it, your email address so we can reply). You can also email us directly. We use
this information only to reply to you and fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer
needed. Google Forms is operated by Google under Google's privacy policy.

## 5. What we don't do

- We don't sell your personal information.
- We don't use your data for anything other than running the app, showing ads as described
  above, and answering your messages.
- The app doesn't request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise
  location.

## 6. Children

The app is meant for people preparing for a driving theory test, generally aged 16 and older.
It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information
from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will
delete it.

## 7. Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Information Google collects for ads
is kept according to Google's retention policies. Bug reports and emails you send us are kept
only as long as needed to handle your request.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Israeli Privacy
Protection Law, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access,
correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and
to withdraw consent. Because we don't keep your study data on our servers, you control it fully
on your device. For ad data, use the in-app **Privacy choices** and your Android ad settings,
or contact Google. For anything else, write to **support.alufhatheoriya@gmail.com**. You may also complain to your
local data protection authority.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at
this address. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
